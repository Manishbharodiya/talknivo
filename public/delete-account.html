Talknivo – Account Deletion Request

Operated by CVR Auto Private Limited

At Talknivo, we respect your privacy and provide a way for users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Talknivo account:

  1. Send an email to bisuashok528@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Talknivo Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the mobile number registered with your Talknivo account, preferably in masked form (for example, +91 XXXXXXX123), so we can begin identifying your account.
  4. Our support team will respond with the steps required to verify account ownership and process your request.

You do not need to provide your OTP, password, or other sensitive authentication credentials.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Following verification, account deletion will cover applicable personal information associated with your Talknivo account, including:

  • Firebase Authentication account information
  • User profile information
  • Stored learning progress and achievements
  • Account-linked preferences and activity records

Some information may be retained where required by applicable law, for security, or for legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Any applicable retention periods depend on the type of information and the relevant requirements.

Important Information

  • Account deletion is permanent once completed.
  • Deleting the Talknivo app from your phone does not automatically delete your account.
  • You may contact us if you need help with your deletion request.

Delete your Talknivo account

Talknivo users must be able to request deletion of their account and associated personal data, including if the app has been uninstalled.

Setup required: The deletion-request email or form is not connected yet. Do not submit this URL to Google Play until a working request channel, verification procedure and deletion workflow are in place.

How the process will work

  1. Send a deletion request through the verified support channel listed on this website.
  2. We verify account ownership using a safe method; never send passwords or OTPs.
  3. We process deletion of the account and associated data, subject to any disclosed legal retention requirements.
  4. We confirm completion using the agreed contact method.

Important

If you have an active subscription, cancellation may need to be handled separately. Details will be provided before launch.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Talknivo, an English-language learning application operated by CVR Auto Private Limited (“Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use Talknivo.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Talknivo Android application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Talknivo, we may collect or process the following information:

Account information

  • Mobile phone number used for authentication
  • Firebase Authentication user identifier
  • Profile information you choose to provide, such as your name, profile image, or selected avatar

Learning and activity information

  • Lesson progress and completed activities
  • Learning preferences and achievements
  • App interaction and usage information
  • Information required to maintain your learning progress across sessions

AI tutor information

If you use an AI-powered learning feature, the messages or questions you submit may be processed to generate responses. Depending on the service configuration, this processing may involve an external AI service provider.

Technical information

Our app and service providers may process technical information necessary for authentication, security, performance, and troubleshooting, such as device information, app version, diagnostic information, and security-related signals.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts
  • Provide English-learning lessons and practice activities
  • Save and display learning progress
  • Personalize the learning experience
  • Provide requested AI-powered features, where available
  • Maintain app functionality and security
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Authentication and Firebase Services

Talknivo uses Google Firebase services for certain application functions, including phone-number authentication and storage of application data.

Firebase Authentication may process phone numbers and information required to verify sign-in requests and prevent abuse.

Other Firebase services may be used to store account information, learning progress, and application content.

For information about Google's handling of data, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

5. AI-Powered Features

If AI-powered features are available in Talknivo, information you enter into those features may be transmitted to the service provider responsible for generating a response.

Please avoid entering passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information into AI conversations.

The specific providers and retention practices applicable to these features should be disclosed when the features are enabled.

6. Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers only as necessary to operate and support Talknivo, such as providers of authentication, cloud infrastructure, application security, and AI functionality.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or to protect the security and integrity of our services.

We do not treat information shared with service providers for application operations as permission for those providers to use it for unrelated purposes.
